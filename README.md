# Yijun Li

Master of Computing in Artificial Intelligence, **National University of Singapore**. Previously at **Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen**.

My research interests include efficient retrieval-augmented generation, reinforcement learning for search agents, and RSI, with a particular focus on evidence utilization, answer quality evaluation, and agent memory.

[Website](https://liyijuncindy.github.io/) · [Email](mailto:liyijun@u.nus.edu) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yijun-li-166a49435/)

## Selected Research

- **Knowledge Graph RAG Evaluation and Optimization** — Undergraduate thesis, advised by Xuefeng Bai. Developed Knowledge Utilization Gap (KUG) and training-free evidence organization. On 500 CWQ questions with Qwen3.5-Flash, improved AnswerHit from 39.6% to 60.2% over direct graph-context answering.
- **State-Local Policy Optimization for Search Agents (SLPO)** — Second author, under review. Contributed to methodology discussions and manuscript writing for action-level credit assignment in search agents.
