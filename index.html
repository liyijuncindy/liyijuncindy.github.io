About

I am a Master of Computing student in Artificial Intelligence at NUS, previously at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen.

Research interests

My research interests include efficient retrieval-augmented generation, reinforcement learning for search agents, and RSI, with a particular focus on evidence utilization, answer quality evaluation, and agent memory.

Selected research

Knowledge Graph RAG Evaluation and Optimization

Undergraduate thesis · Advisor: Xuefeng Bai

Developed Knowledge Utilization Gap (KUG) and training-free evidence organization. On 500 CWQ questions with Qwen3.5-Flash, improved AnswerHit from 39.6% to 60.2% over direct graph-context answering.

State-Local Policy Optimization for Search Agents

SLPO · Second author · Research manuscript

Action-level credit assignment for search agents through comparisons under the same retrieval history. Contributed to methodology discussions and manuscript writing.
