About

I’m studying for a Master of Computing in AI at NUS, after my undergrad in Computer Science at HIT Shenzhen. These days I think about how language models and search agents can make better use of the evidence they retrieve.

My interests: efficient retrieval-augmented generation, reinforcement learning for search agents, and recursive self-improvement (RSI), with a particular focus on how models utilize retrieved evidence, how answer quality is evaluated, and how agents manage memory.

  • Retrieval-augmented generation
  • Search agents & RL
  • Evidence utilization
  • Answer quality evaluation
  • Agent memory

Knowledge Graph RAG Evaluation and Optimization

Undergraduate thesis
